    About this project

    What it recognises
    Five classes: thumb, pointer, middle finger, ring finger and pinky.
    How it's built
    An image model trained with Google Teachable Machine, running in your browser with TensorFlow.js.
    Your images
    Photos and camera frames are processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
    Limits
    The model only knows those five classes. It always picks one, even when there's no finger in the picture or several are showing, so check the confidence before trusting a result.
