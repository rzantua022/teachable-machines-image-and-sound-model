    About this project

    Image model
    Recognises five fingers from a photo or the camera: thumb, pointer, middle finger, ring finger and pinky.
    Sound model
    Listens through the microphone and recognises eight animals: cat, cow, dog, duck, owl, pig, sheep and tuko. It also has a class for background noise.
    How it's built
    Both models were trained with Google Teachable Machine and run in your browser with TensorFlow.js.
    Your data
    Photos, camera frames and microphone audio are processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
    Limits
    Each model only knows its own classes. It always picks one, even when none of them fit, so check the confidence before trusting a result.
